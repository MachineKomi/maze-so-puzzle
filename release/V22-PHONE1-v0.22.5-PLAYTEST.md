## What changed

- Short landscape gameplay keeps the minimap, Friends/Bag, feedback and direction
  pad separate. Longer instructional feedback fits without being hidden.
- Short-height Adventure Book keeps its header/tabs in place while the collection
  scrolls; tab words stay whole and a complete achievement card fits.
- Windows Title Exit closes the actual native window/process, with browser-safe
  guidance when native closing is unavailable. Play stays usable.
- The praised desktop/iPad composition and v0.22.4 stationary rescue, pace,
  independent volume settings and Dolphin repair are retained.

This is a bounded UI increment, not completion of every phone screen or the UI
programme. Home/dialog/victory refinements, HUD pace/settings artwork and calibrated
75/75 audio defaults are still forthcoming. It does not change camera behavior,
FOV, puzzle rules, level content, reward rules or assets. Physical iPad camera
stutter remains unresolved; no new performance success is claimed.
