## New in this build

- **Chill / Regular / Zippy** movement in Sound & comfort. Chill is slower,
  Regular a little slower than the previous default, Zippy faster. The setting
  survives reopening and resetting progress; puzzle rules are unchanged.
- **Music / Sound effects** sliders, shared mute and Test sound. Start at the
  unchanged intended22% music/100% effects mix, then choose what suits your device.
  Lower music if effects are difficult to hear. Adjusting a level does not restart
  the song. Settings stay local to each device.
- Tessera Dolphin's field tail and flippers now use the repaired transparent
  derivative, retaining the approved design and placement.

## Please check when convenient

1. Let Alex/Ame try Chill and Regular; try Zippy yourself. Do taps, holds and turns
   feel controllable? Does changing pace stop old input cleanly?
2. On iPad at comfortable low system volume, lower Music and use Test sound,
   then hear a pickup, door, rescue and battle. Are effects clear without being
   harsh? Check mute/unmute and reopening retain your chosen balance.
3. Background and return to the app; tap if audio needs activation. Report any
   stuck silence, delayed burst, or a blip when Quick Mute/Next is the first tap.
4. In Maze9 Springstep Sky Hollow (or a later encounter), check the Dolphin's
   tail/flippers in its cage and following Ame. Book art should look unchanged.
5. On Windows, play a few steps, close with **X or Alt+F4**, reopen and Continue.
   Your run and settings should remain. Title Exit has a known separate issue.
