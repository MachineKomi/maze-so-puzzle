# FP-ART-OST v0.20.1 corrective family playtest

This is the corrected Art & OST Preview. It closes the presentation and asset
coverage issues found while reviewing 0.20.0, before the larger Plan 01 UI/UX
overhaul. It is still an unsigned prototype, not a store-ready release.

## Launch

Prefer `Maze-so-Puzzle-0.20.1-portable.exe`. Windows SmartScreen may warn
because the preview is unsigned. Verify its SHA-256 against
`FP-ART-OST-v0.20.1-manifest.json` or `SHA256SUMS.txt` before bypassing any
warning. The installer contains the same build but has not been tested on a
separate clean machine.

The preview keeps the separate `com.ame.mazesopuzzle.preview` application-data
namespace used by 0.20.0.

## Suggested ten-minute journey

1. Start on the new illustrated title screen and use Play to enter Home.
2. Inspect the repaired Ame-and-friends Home hero edge and subtle glow.
3. Begin Chapters 1 and 3 to see the refreshed Professor Poggle and Sprig
   portraits.
4. Use the tester picker to open a teleporter maze and confirm there is no
   redundant white suit glyph beside a flower pad.
5. Open a keyed door and confirm Ame stays visible and stationary while the
   longer door magic and sound play.
6. Open the Adventure Book and try Default, Expand, and Collapse on the denser
   friend roster.
7. Start several Surprise Mazes and look for the expanded ordinary/mythic friend
   pool, including Green Tea Skeleton and Rainbow Horn Unicorn.

## Known boundaries

- Plan 01 still owns the comprehensive HUD/layout, typography, modal and
  cross-device interface overhaul.
- The current authored 16-maze campaign retains its curated existing friend
  placements; Plan 09 owns intentional placement of the expanded roster.
- Lighting, wider VFX, controller support, animation, campaign expansion, co-op,
  Friend Garden and final story/voice work remain later roadmap stages.
- The build is unsigned, and physical iPad/TV/phone and clean-machine installer
  checks remain external gates.
