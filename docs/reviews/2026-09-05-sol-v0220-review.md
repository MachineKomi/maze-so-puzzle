## 1. Outcome

I agree with Astra's central assessment and proposed ownership. v0.22.0 is a
real product improvement and its successful desktop/iPad visual system is now a
regression baseline, not permission for another broad redesign. It is not yet a
fully accepted family preview because sustained iPad play can become nearly
unplayable, the phone layout does not fit its short landscape height, and live
held input is discarded by successful interactions.

The next work should therefore be two bounded, separately reviewable tranches:

1. **V22-PERF-01:** Astra owns sustained-play profiling, evidenced hot-path
   reductions, truthful scene-quality relief and held-input/blocker correctness;
   Sol reviews read-only before promotion.
2. **V22-UI-01:** Sol owns short-height layout and the requested Bestiary,
   thumb-pad, pickup, Book and victory polish; Astra reviews input/performance
   safety. This starts only after V22-PERF-01 and any required deeper
   renderer-isolation tranche are reviewed and accepted.

Claude is not needed again before measured results exist. Agent 04 remains held.

## 2. Evidence and authority

I read the complete Human transcript, both complete imported Opus reports,
Astra's assessment, the 88-row ledger, the current joint state and the relevant
UI/performance/controls contracts. I inspected the supplied desktop, iPad and
phone evidence; a Sol-side independent reviewer inspected all 51 images.

Evidence is separated as follows:

- **Human authority:** reported experience, desired outcome and visual/product
  preference. These requirements are binding even when a proposed cause is

- **Observed repository fact:** current code, tests, CSS and documented release

The Human's strongest positive evidence is unusually clear: preserve the warm

## 3. Independent repository checks

| Finding | Sol assessment |
| --- | --- |
| MiniMap memoization | The component is memoized, but `App.tsx` supplies a fresh filtered objects array and, outside exploration, a newly constructed full-level Set. Stabilizing meaningful inputs is a high-confidence near-term correction. |
| Board-drag cursor | Coordinate motion publishes `touchCursor` through App state; same-direction pointer movement can therefore commit far more of the tree than its semantic intent requires. The fixed ThumbPad does not have exactly the same defect. |
| Scene-travel binding | `useSceneTravel` has an unscoped layout effect and repeats DOM discovery/binding work on commits. Instrument first; split node discovery from lightweight retargeting only with exact rescue/follower/resize/discontinuity tests. |
| Held intent | `clearHeldInput()` erases keyboard order, d-pad identity and board pointer capture/state. Successful battle, rescue, portal and door paths call it, so a still-held finger cannot resume. This is an implementation defect against the newly explicit Human contract. |
| Fresh blocker explanation | Too-strong feedback shows its explanatory modal only on the first bump. Human authority now requires one explanation for every fresh deliberate failed attempt, while one continuous blocked hold must not flood. |
| Finale | Normal completion already enters `makeSurprise()`. The modulo wrap is tester-only. Reproduce normal, replay and tester paths before changing the correct normal branch. |
| Quality relief | `lite` has no meaningful scene recipe; it mostly alters surface tokens/modal blur. Static disables many animations and ordinary travel. The Human's Lite + reduced-motion test therefore did not exercise a credible low-cost scene path. |
| Responsive cause | Current layout and Book CSS do use height. The phone failure is poorly coordinated minima, optical sizes and short-height rules—not literally width-only responsiveness. Claude's sample global-scale expression is not implementation-ready and must not shrink hit targets. |
| Persistence | Ordinary run updates synchronously normalize/serialize/write active-run state. Include this as an independently measured sustained-route cohort; do not batch or weaken durability without an explicit loss-window decision. |
| Filter/animation cost | Filters, terrain, followers and infinite motion are credible suspects, not a proven single GPU diagnosis. Isolate them. Never bake directional cast shadows into canonical sprites; that conflicts with the Art Bible. |
| Victory | The screen still reads as several equal-weight receipt bands. On phone it scrolls, which also conflicts with the locked no-scroll requirement. A larger friend hero and living-but-bounded celebration are mandatory in the UI tranche, not optional future decoration. |

## 4. Claude disposition

### Adopt now, bounded to the next two tranches

- Profile matched fresh/sustained large-maze routes by input surface and follower
  count; stabilize MiniMap inputs, cursor commits and evidenced travel binding.
- Give Lite a real scene-cost reduction without changing puzzle information or
  sacrificing Full-quality appearance.
- Preserve physical held intent through eligible successful presentations;
  require a fresh press after a genuine block; prevent accidental selection.
- Implement the Human's exact Bestiary states, open pad chevrons, short-height
  phone fit, current Surprise dimensions, lore navigation, Book optical polish,
  larger desktop pickup feedback and materially happier no-scroll victory.

### Adapt or test later

- Compare camera follow, cadence, pixel snapping, compositor hints, containment,
  cached terrain, contextual sound and ambient motion one variable at a time.
- Rectangular camera windows may be a useful later experiment, but are not
  necessary to prove the requested same-layout/smaller-phone-elements outcome.
- Runtime shadow underlays or lower-tier filter removal are valid trials; edited
  canonical art with baked directional shadows is not.
- Combat arithmetic is mostly present (`before + enemy = after`). Remaining work
  is the explicit deficit/action wording and presentation receipt, not another
  combat-system overhaul.

### Reject for the current programme

- Claude's adaptive 9–15-tile default. The Human-approved future preference is
  4/5/6/7, default 6, under PT32/Plan 08.
- A universal two-action dialog limit, consumable keys, Power spending, early
  economy work or hiding wanted keepsake totals.
- A broad diegetic HUD or decorative minimap redesign. The Human likes these
  surfaces; reopen only if later Human/child evidence gives a reason.
- Claims that generated mazes are universally trees, that every static filter is
  rerasterized every frame, or that baking terrain/shadows is free.
- An arbitrary `App.tsx` line-count target, a single mega-clock for rules/CSS/audio,
  or moving Plan 10 beyond the release candidate.
- Applying Bestiary's hidden-unknown policy to Friends. The Human likes the
  Friends collection as it is.

## 5. Differences and refinements to Astra's first assessment

Astra covered the complete Human intake. The following refinements are material:

1. Record adaptive 9–15 FOV and broad HUD/minimap redesigns as rejected for this
   programme, not merely ambient deferred debt.
2. Add synchronous active-run persistence to the performance causal matrix,
   while explicitly forbidding cadence/durability changes in V22-PERF-01.
3. Split Claude's shadow proposal: reject baking casts into approved sprites;
   retain measured runtime underlay/filter-tier experiments.
4. Treat held continuation as specified behaviour with an implementation defect,
   not an open product choice.
5. Make no-scroll victory, larger friend hero and ongoing bounded celebration a
   required part of the second tranche.
6. Use the liked mobile pickup prominence as the direction of travel: enlarge
   desktop; do not average the problem by shrinking phone.
7. Do not enlarge shared iPad components until the phone rule passes and the
   already-good desktop/iPad composition is shown side by side.

## 6. Acceptance split

### V22-PERF-01 — Astra writes, Sol reviews

The exact build-ready contract is
[V22-PERF-01](../plans/V22-PERF-01-sustained-play-and-live-input.md). It owns
measurement, mechanical hot-path reductions, truthful Lite relief, eligible held

content, persistence batching, media or broad effects.

The result must be technically reviewed before promotion. A physical older-iPad
same-route fresh/sustained comparison remains the decisive Human gate. If the
bounded work does not materially help, do not decorate the failure: run a second
renderer-isolation tranche before changing phone layout.

### [V22-UI-01](../plans/V22-UI-01-short-height-and-reward.md) — Sol writes, Astra reviews

Preserve the current square-board/product topology. At normal text size, short
landscape gameplay must expose the board plus Power/currencies, objective,
minimap, feedback, Hint, More and pad without document/deck scrolling or clipping.
Collection bodies may scroll, but their header/tabs must fit and at least one
complete card must be visible. Home, short lore/settings and victory fit; long
story copy may have one bounded body scroller. Victory never scrolls.

The UI tranche also owns the exact Human-requested Bestiary states, code-native
open chevrons with unchanged hit sectors, current Surprise dimensions, lore
previous/next and swipe, Book icon/tab/card optics, tactile pointer treatment,
larger desktop pickup feedback and the required victory hierarchy/celebration.
