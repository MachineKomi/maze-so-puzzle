## What the game is trying to achieve

Maze so Puzzle should make a child want to explore, notice something, try an idea,
understand what changed and enjoy the result. Beautiful readable art and sound
make the world inviting; responsive movement preserves trust; fair puzzles make
progress feel earned; friends, lore and a truthful Book make discoveries memorable.
Quiet planning gives the larger reward moments room to feel special.

The Human's wish for an engaging, compelling game is served by curiosity,
voluntary replay, mastery and collection. It does not authorize punishment,
compulsive schedules, paid chance, artificial friction or forced resource farming.
Cosy does not mean no decisions: comparison, sequence, reusable tools, portals,
spatial memory and changed-state returns can produce meaningful inference with
the existing forgiving rules. Educational intent is an outcome to observe, not
a claim established by solvability, a test count or adding a quiz.
