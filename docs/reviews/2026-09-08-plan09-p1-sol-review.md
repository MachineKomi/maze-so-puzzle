## Final bounded disposition

I support closing Plan09-P1 as an isolated engineering and observation comparison. The compact first-use fixture is the stronger primary canary: its required weapon, Power-9 blocker and reachable Power-5 growth answer are readable together, the return is short, and the payoff includes the named defeat plus entry onto the newly opened tile. The room-return fixture is a useful secondary canary for remembering a changed relationship between rooms. Its desktop clue deliberately relies on the preceding view for the Power-5 room, so observation should distinguish remembered relation from simultaneous visual comparison.

There is no production-content blocker in the frozen P1 packet. Its current limits are explicit and acceptable for this tranche: the friend is a one-input optional interaction, raw topology metrics are not reasoning scores, the Gold symbol may be confused with the goal, checkpoint captures do not prove live journey cadence, and the Power-10 Lanternlight room is not an underpowered comparison. I do not recommend promoting either map from this evidence alone. A voluntary family observation should compare the two canaries and current references, record unaided action, Hint use and adult help separately, and retain the Human's preference for varied rooms and readable spatial relationships when a later production proposal is made.
