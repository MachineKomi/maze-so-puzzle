## Scope and resulting behaviour

The engine still commits exact cardinal tile moves. Collision, reachability,
currency, saves, six-tile default view and input cadence are unchanged. One
ref-owned requestAnimationFrame loop samples a bounded orthogonal presentation
path for Ame, the camera and the cosmetic friend procession. React renders on
ordinary state changes; travel does not schedule per-frame React updates.

`src/tileTravel.ts` owns the pure clock/path. Taps take112ms. Held repeats use the
existing next-repeat interval (260ms toward160ms); the deliberate320ms first-hold
pause remains. Retargeting consumes elapsed travel before accepting the next
committed waypoint, retaining turns instead of drawing a diagonal chord. Early
reversals discard an unpainted excursion. Catch-up speed bounds remaining travel
to `min(280ms, duration+32ms)` at every accepted retarget. No predicted steps or
new analogue movement are introduced. A stalled frame consumes elapsed time;
it cannot add engine moves.

`src/ui/game/useSceneTravel.ts` owns CSS `translate` on the world, player,
replacement battle actor, camera-anchored effects and follower nodes. Existing
pose `transform` and VFX timelines remain separate. It caches board content size
through ResizeObserver; no layout reads or DOM queries occur in its frame loop.
The exported ref snapshot supplies rendered actor/camera coordinates, pending
camera bounds, board content size and follower positions. Pointer/touch targeting
uses that content box and its border origin. World objects cover the complete
painted/pending camera envelope plus the newly committed view and a one-tile
visual gutter, while authoritative exploration still controls eligibility.
A single anchored pickup notice remains mounted until its own deadline and is
clipped at the actual board edge. Treasure flights capture the displayed camera
at collection and retain that measured screen-space origin while they fly.

PT40 replaces coordinate deduplication/camera-based assignment with bounded24
breadcrumb entries and stable friend slots. Repeated visits remain in order;
off-camera friends stay on the actual corridor. New/resumed friends gather and
then unspool behind Ame. Portals/jumps explicitly gather at landing and hide the
ordinary procession while the existing effect owns the transition. No gameplay
pathfinder, persistent follower schema or reward change is added.

## Boundaries and downstream ownership

- Ordinary door/combat may begin during the tail of an already committed
  approach. That approach completes on the same clock; the stationary interaction
  adds no movement into the door/enemy. The replacement battle actor inherits
  Ame's travel offset. Existing presentation locks/cancellation remain intact.
- Jump/portal/run changes are explicit discontinuities. Modal entry, navigation,
  hidden page, blur, resize and unmount cancel the active frame and settle to
  committed state. A later visible page cannot replay missed movement.
- Full and Reduced motion share the necessary smooth travel clock; Reduced
  removes decorative poses/effects. Manual quality Static settles immediately.
  Static is an available alternative, not a claim that tile snapping is more
  comfortable for this family.
- Plan04 mounts world lighting with the world and consumes this coordinate
  contract. Plan02 owns effect timelines, Plan05 owns local sprite poses, Plan08
  owns normalized input and future zoom. None adds a second travel owner.
- Plan07B later qualifies frame cost and retained resources. Camera zoom PT32,
  new mechanics/rooms PT43/44, materials and later Book/victory work are excluded
  from this bounded checkpoint and remain in their assigned plans.
