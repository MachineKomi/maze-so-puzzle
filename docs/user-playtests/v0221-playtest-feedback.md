# Feedback on v0221

Okay, I did play test the new build quickly. I tested it on my mobile phone, and I tested it on the iPad. For context, the iPad is an eighth generation iPad, so generation number eight iPad, and it's got 32 gigabytes of storage, but it does and can successfully play lots of different 2D games, you know, video streaming, stuff that should, like games with physics and lots going on, all buttery smooth on the device. So it should definitely be possible for us to optimize this type of 2D game, even once we've added more effects and some 2D physics and stuff, to run smoothly on that kind of device. So the findings were, obviously I wasn't able to test the desktop build since there was no Tori desktop build created for version 0.2.2.1. But I did test build version 0.2.2.1 on my mobile phone and on the iPad. The findings were: on my mobile phone, everything was buttery smooth. Obviously none of the, like, mobile UI jank has been resolved, but I'm sure that's in the roadmap. That's fine. But it was buttery smooth. It did feel like an improvement on mobile. Like it just felt really good. The movement was really smooth. Everything just worked great. Music was loading, like straight away. Everything seemed good. On the iPad, everything was fine on the very first maze, so, like the very small maze where you see the whole maze. It was buttery smooth, everything responsive, great. Also on the iPad, the music seemed to be loading with no delays or anything, so that was great. But as soon as I went even to the second maze in the campaign, which gets a bit bigger, it was, I would describe it as laggy as fuck. Like lagging, lagging so bad that, again, you just have to move one step at a time, and even then it looks pretty, pretty not good. So performance optimization: totally successful for mobile. I'm assuming it's probably fine on desktop, but I haven't tested it on desktop because there was no Tori build. On iPad, we are still, or at least on this old generation eight iPad, we're still having pretty, pretty bad performance. Lots of lag. But when the movement is working correctly, it is very nice and smooth. It looks great, feels great when you don't have lag. And I'm excited to see the three different movement speeds: the chill, normal walking speed, and zippy speed. Further clarification on performance on iPad. All of the menus and other UI interactions on iPad, very responsive, very quick, no lag, no issues. It's purely the movement in the mazes, and even the animation of the door opening on iPad in the maze, even on a big maze, that's lagging, but, like, the little door opening animation is fine. Also, the animation of the dog, like, rescuing the friends out of the cages, that animation is perfect, looks good, and the pickup. So it feels like the only thing that's lagging now on the iPad. Yeah, even the fighting, even the battle animations and stuff on the iPad, all perfect and great and, you know, looks great, like as if nothing's wrong. The only thing that feels bad on the iPad is the movement. It's just that the movement doesn't feel smooth. And because we've got this nice smooth movement now, it feels, again, not as bad as when it was, like, the jerky tile-based camera movement, but it just feels like lag. It just feels laggy when you move the character around the maze on iPad, which sucks. I'm curious to know whether the walking speed or the slow, the chill speed movement, if that actually resolves the issue on iPad. But even actually moving just one tile forward, still just, I don't know, it's like there's a little bit of delay of the camera trying to catch up or something. It just doesn't feel right. But everything else on the iPad in terms of performance seems fine. But obviously, you know, as we continue to add complexity, more effects, the friend garden, more mechanics, stuff like that, more beautiful graphics with the, like, 3D effect on the walls and the better lighting and everything, we need to be very, very mindful and conscious throughout the roadmap and throughout implementation and delivery that we are optimizing as much as possible and that we are delivering the best graphics possible, the best quality sprites possible, but also the best performance.

I forgot to add - I tried turning off the animations and turning surface quality down (like turning all of the graphics options we have in the settings panel down to minimum) and it seemed to very slightly make movement and camera smoother on the ipad but it didn't completely resolve it - still not the same lovely smooth movement we get on desktop and my Samsung S25 plus+ or whatever my phone is.
