## `hole02-a`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production-candidate ground-hole sprite for the original child-friendly 2D game Maze so Puzzle. Create a brand-new image, not an edit. A single deep, empty round pit seen in strict top-down orthographic view, centered on a genuinely transparent background, with 10% transparent padding on every side. The pit has a simple softly irregular rounded opening, a narrow smooth dusty-lavender stone/earth lip, one broad mauve inner sidewall band, and a large very dark aubergine void. It must unambiguously look dug DOWN into the ground, not a raised bowl, ring, portal, donut, rock pile, or crater mountain. Depth is shown by a calm concentric value progression, with the center dark and opaque. No visible bottom and no stepping stones. Clean chunky cel-painterly anime storybook game craft: 2–4 large masses, three broad value groups, bright material-local plum/mauve boundary colours, crisp tidy antialiased silhouette. Neutral diffuse light evenly around the edge so the sprite can rotate without moving a baked light source. Restrained colour palette: dusty lavender rim, warm mauve inner wall, deep aubergine center. The stone/earth lip has at most three broad joint notches and a few broad plane changes, no pebble gravel, no scratches or small rocky ledges. Prioritize instant readability when only 48 pixels tall. No white sticker outline, no black outer contour, no cast shadow outside the pit, no glow, sparkles, decoration, plants, characters, text, logos, scene, checkerboard, or coloured matte. Real RGBA transparency outside the entire pit; the dark pit interior remains opaque. Native square image, entire outline in frame.
