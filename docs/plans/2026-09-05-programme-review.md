## Creative direction within the approved programme

Prefer a few recognizable, well-timed moments over more simultaneous activity:

- A familiar landmark becomes useful from its other side; a short loop opens;
  a predicted landing reveals the value of a clue. These are level compositions
  using existing rules, not new abilities or a new landmark system.
- Ame notices, guesses and corrects alongside her friends. Short exchanges can
  show her agency while keeping clues inspectable outside dialogue.
- Keep ordinary movement calm. Let the encounter, rescue or discovery own the
  expressive beat, then give the player room to make the next decision.
- Use material contrast and coherent grounding to make a maze feel like a place,
  while preserving the same readable path in lower-quality/reduced-motion modes.
- Give optional co-op roles reasons to notice and help each other. Test whether
  stealing/keep-away produces shared laughter rather than excluding one player.
- Make existing rewards feel acknowledged through exact receipts, earned detail
  and truthful story. Persistent XP, new traversal abilities, sticker editing
  and voices remain later hypotheses with their existing decision gates.
