## Outcome and inherited implementation

Pickups look worth investigating, a reward is easy to read, friends celebrate
with personality, and a large earned sticker gets a moment of admiration.
Quiet exploration stays calm; celebrations contrast with it and end promptly.
Preserve the [Human discovery/celebration intake](../playtests/2026-09-06-depth-discovery-and-celebration.md),
[VFX Bible](../VFX_BIBLE.md), [UI-02](UI-02-adventure-book-and-focus-polish.md),
motion/sound preferences and current exact-once completion/reward contracts.

VFX-02A is already live on web0.22.10: one bounded 24/12-token Canvas, wall bounce,
presented-player homing, trails and grouped sounds for committed Gold/Science/
maze-Power. Arithmetic combat/potion feedback, distinct friend victory signatures
and earned-sticker detail cards also exist. Extend those owners, not another
renderer, clock, completion trigger or currency. Native0.22.10 remains unqualified.

## Three small checkpoints, each independently reviewable

### A. Pickup presence and readable receipts

- Keep a visible, calm baseline glow throughout the breath cycle; avoid the
  reported blink/off reading. Start with unchanged approved pickup art and a
  static backing plus bounded opacity/scale accents; verify the actual composite.
- Full may add restrained occasional twinkles. Lite retains useful presence
  with cheaper decoration; Reduced/Static retain clear static emphasis. No
  invisible pickups, flicker, animated blur/filter-list mismatch or new per-item
  continuous JS timer. Quiet tiles need no motion to prove that the game is alive.
- Preserve strong phone feedback and enlarge desktop receipts only where the
  source/geometry comparison demonstrates the reported small reading. Exact
  values remain readable during and after the shower, including large values.
- Freeze active-effect/node/canvas and decoded-memory limits against the current
  director before implementation. Stop decorative work offscreen/hidden and on
  resize, navigation or cancellation; prove no accumulation across repeated use.

### B. Friend-led victory with truthful rewards

- Make current rescued friends the main celebration: larger readable staging,
  distinct purposeful gestures, occasional short flips for suitable species and
  finite fireworks/confetti. Use existing approved art and director initially;
  new authored sprite sequences stay in Plan05. Keep faces and reward text clear.
- A normal completion with no optional rescues or with requested Hint help still
  deserves a complete warm celebration. Do not imply failure, shame or lost
  learning value. Optional perfect play may add a distinct flourish.
- Preserve no-scroll ordinary victory composition and reachable Stay/Next.
  Effects never delay the decision or seize input; reduced/static replaces flight,
  flipping and flashes with attractive stable poses and the same information.
- Resolve **PT10** explicitly in copy and staging: projected completion rewards
  are not already banked. Stay -> Book shows the true durable state; reentering
  the star -> Next -> reopen commits once. Explain when a medal/reward will be
  recorded without changing the existing banking transaction in this tranche.
  Flying tokens, sound completion and popup dismissal cannot become award owners.

### C. Earned-sticker fanfare during maze play

- Consume newly committed, eligible achievement events from the existing award
  owner. Show the actual large approved sticker in a finite, legible flourish
  with short original copy and coordinated sound. Use existing earned stickers;
  do not manufacture the three future collection awards before Plan09/10.
- The adapter must originate after a **successful durable progress write**.
  Victory's projected `newStickerIds`/`newMedalIds`/`newBadgeIds` are not earned
  events. Stay, failed saves and unsupported-profile in-memory continuation
  cannot emit an earned fanfare. Existing projected victory sound is not proof
  of a commit. Freeze the event/commit/context table against current source;
  do not move completion banking earlier to force a mid-maze animation. A
  Next-committed event can present at the next safe scene boundary; future09/10
  awards use the same adapter at their own real commit boundary.
- Freeze a bounded queue/coalescing rule for simultaneous awards. Preserve every
  durable achievement in the Book even when decorative playback is skipped.
  Restart, reload, background/resume and revisiting the Book must not replay old
  award events. If no suitable event exists, root defines the smallest adapter
  with the existing progress owner; no polling store or parallel receipt ledger.
- Keep the player, route, blocker explanation and immediate controls readable.
  Queue behind critical dialogs; support dismissal/skip if needed through the
  existing semantic actions. No new modal that traps movement or interrupts a
  required explanation. Follow the current overlay/input suspension contract.
- Respect mute, SFX gain and the existing audio bus/voice cap. Do not introduce
  a compressor, new audio asset pack or whole-mix retune as a prerequisite.

## Qualification and stop rule

Before each checkpoint, capture its current counterpart and name the one visible
improvement. Measure budget allocation and compare the same scene/profile, idle
and moving, Full/Lite/Reduced/Static. Reuse the shared harness, not a second
physics/director system or broad gallery of speculative variants.

Acceptance covers small/large awards, narrow/open rooms, moving pickup/homing,
one/many/no victory friends, multiple achievements, large text/compact windows,
all interaction sources currently supported, hidden page, mute, resize, navigation,
cancel, Stay/Next/reopen and repeated lifecycle use. Check exact reward arithmetic,
focus/return, no overlap of key text or player, no unbounded work and no frame-time
regression concealed by static screenshots. A no-motion version must remain lovely.

Root owns resource budgets, full ready-slice checks, spec updates and release
qualification; Sol checks source and actual rendered states independently.
Family evidence asks whether the moment is understandable and satisfying, whether
the Book reward expectation is correct, and whether exploration remains calm.
Record pending hardware honestly; compilation or browser case count proves
neither iPad comfort nor Windows release acceptance.

After A/B/C, stop this tranche and advance LEARN-01. Route living goal spiral,
poison variation and additional event polish to their existing Plan02 backlog
cards; join hazard rendering with HOLE-02/04 consumers where appropriate. Do not
let unbounded ambient polish delay the learning canary, controls or campaign.
Rollback each checkpoint as a complete presentation change without removing
earned progress. Log evidence/build outputs and retain the tested predecessor.
