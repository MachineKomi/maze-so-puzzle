## Required presentation

### Safe Power comparison and useful next action

- Retain current player and guardian numbers, identity and the true comparison.
  Add concise exact shortfall `max(0, enemyPower - playerPower)`, such as
  "Need 3 more Power". Equality wins; never invent an extra point or damage.
- Preserve the weapon prerequisite. Enough Power alone does not promise victory
  without the required weapon. Shortfall describes maze Power, never permanent XP.
- Keep one clear safe-return sentence and only witnessed current opportunities.
  Show a known gain where useful and truthful; do not imply one small gain alone
  solves the deficit. A reachable opportunity is not necessarily an immediate step.
- Reuse existing opportunity authority. No stale card appears during asynchronous
  replacement; cancellation stops work. An exhausted/empty bounded search means
  "ask for Required Path help", not proof that no opportunity exists.
- Preserve fresh deliberate re-explanation and continuous-hold flood protection.
  Dismissal restores focus and cannot replay movement or auto-open another hint.

### Exact gain and result

- Retain readable before + gained = after for every real potion/battle event and
  a settled terminal receipt. Grouped representative tokens never change amounts.
- During combat transfer, presented player gain equals presented guardian loss;
  their sum remains the initial total. End at the exact committed player total
  and zero remaining transferred guardian Power, using the existing event/plan.
- No new award, random draw, input lock or combat retiming is needed for teaching.
  Hidden-page/navigation/cancel/reload reconciles to authoritative state; neither
  partial numbers nor flying-token arrival can erase, repeat or delay credit.
- Distinguish current-run Power from projected completion rewards. LEARN-01 does
  not change Stay/Next banking; the victory owner keeps provisional/earned copy true.

### Picture-led, request-only four-tier hints

- Preserve the existing sequence: required goal, relevant principle, orienting
  landmark/direction, then explicit next step. Simplify each to one short main
  thought with optional supporting text, retaining exact rules and useful meaning.
- Use approved generic rule/tool pictures or already legitimately known identity
  where safe. Add no unknown guardian/friend name, art variant, location, count,
  portal connection or unexplored-map disclosure beyond that requested tier.
  A generic semantic icon is the fallback; never fetch hidden presentation art.
- First two tiers retain their absence of target IDs/directions. Later markers
  keep current tier/discovery rules. Do not advance discovery or reveal fog from help.
- Keep exactly four tiers and current per-state progression/reset semantics.
  Required Path continues to derive from the engine-valid zero-rescue route;
  optional rescue/treasure never becomes the required next goal.
- Keep hints opt-in, revisitable and dismissible. No automatic escalation, quiz,
  forced lesson, new tutorial screen, automatic move or path-spoiler overlay.
  Preserve true single-hole landings, reusable keys and persistent paired portals.
