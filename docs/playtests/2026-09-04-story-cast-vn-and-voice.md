# Playtest intake — story cast, compact VN interludes, and future voice study

Date: 2026-09-04

Source: direct Human project-author request while reviewing v0.20.0.

## Requested outcomes

- Revisit the existing story cast before the expanded campaign is finalized:
  retain, refine, replace, or add characters according to the final world,
  gameplay arc, and Amelia-facing tone.
- Explore replacing each single-speaker story card with a very short visual-
  novel-style exchange. A normal interlude should be only two or three dialogue
  turns, may include Ame replying, and must remain easy to advance or skip with
  a few taps, clicks, keys, or controller actions.
- Later explore AI-generated voice acting for those compact interludes and for
  selected tutorial guidance.

## Product interpretation

The compact dialogue proposal is a narrative-content and presentation feature,
not permission to turn the maze game into a long-form visual novel. Story must
remain warm, concise, child-readable, optional to replay, and mechanically
non-blocking. Cast changes should precede final key art and recorded dialogue.

Voice work is not approved production scope yet. It needs a separate feasibility
and ethics/licensing review covering performer consent and likeness, child-safe
delivery, pronunciation, local/offline packaging, download size, accessibility,
subtitles, volume/mute controls, fallback when media fails, and whether synthetic
voices improve the family experience enough to justify those costs.

## Routing

- Plan 01 supplies a reusable accessible story-dialogue shell and focus/skip
  semantics without rewriting the current story.
- Plan 09 owns cast/canon review, the final 24-chapter narrative arc, and any
  implementation of bounded two-to-three-turn interludes.
- Plan 11 consumes the accepted final cast for presentation/key art.
- Plan 14 performs the planning-only voice-acting opportunity review after the
  dialogue scripts and integrated experience are stable. Voice generation or
  runtime publication requires a later explicit Human-approved plan.
