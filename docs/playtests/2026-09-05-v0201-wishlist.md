## Mimic policy clarification — supersedes future rigid global probability

The Human is revising the earlier fixed 65% good / 35% Mimic proposal. The game
designer must control a versioned placement/policy profile. The exact new odds
and Power/drop ranges are implementation design decisions, not supplied numbers.
All families still share one mechanic and one audited economy; arbitrary
per-sprite code paths are not wanted.

- A chest on a required route can be guaranteed benign. Allow a randomized
  required-path encounter only with a proof that **every allowed outcome** is
  passable using power/resources reachable before that choke point. Optional-only
  Mimics remain the initial conservative generated policy.
- An optional Mimic may be too strong at first encounter, with truthful guidance
  and safe retreat. It must be beatable using a valid attainable route elsewhere
  in that same maze, without requiring its own reward or items behind itself.
  Use the actual engine combat comparison, including equality, rather than an
  invented stricter rule or naive total of every mutually exclusive reward.
- The reveal result stays deterministic across repeat bumps/reload/resume. Do
  not reroll or quietly rescale an already revealed foe to the player's current
  Power. Historical reward-rule versions and pinned saved content keep their
  meanings. New placement, policy and economy require explicit versioning.
- A defeated Mimic **always** awards more loot than its comparable ordinary
  chest. Freeze the comparator before implementation: currency-specific bounds
  or another explicit designer-approved economy comparison; do not claim two
  unlike currencies can simply be added. A lucky normal chest must not exceed
  the promised minimum Mimic defeat reward under that comparison.
- A large Mimic reveal and short acting/effect beat should make the surprise
  legible before combat, without mandatory camera zoom, frightening jump-scare
  intensity, obscured Power or a long repeated input lock. Existing presentation
  art and motion modes apply.
- Gold/Science showers can continue homing to the collector while movement
  resumes. The wallet commits once independently of particle arrival. Moving,
  turning, transitions, cancellation or muted sound cannot lose or duplicate
  rewards. Pool visual representatives; use a bounded, varied, softly mixed
  collection sound that remains pleasant during repeated large showers.
- Gold gets restrained gold glint; Science uses the existing approved semantic
  palette, with blue/green as the Human's suggested direction if consistent.
  Holographic-rainbow XP crystals remain conditional on a separately accepted
  persistent progression design in Plan 14. No XP currency/system is authorized
  merely by describing its possible appearance here.

## Read-only leads, distinct from confirmed causes

At planning HEAD `09413c175c5776044e4ab5dd6eddf31804d33d34`, during Agent 01's
uncommitted return, read-only reviewers found:

- `src/game/followerTrail.ts` removes repeated breadcrumb coordinates;
  `getVisibleFollowerPoints` filters by camera before the renderer assigns friend
  indices. That can change a friend's assigned breadcrumb when the viewport
  moves. This is a concrete code lead for PT40, not completed browser proof.
- Bubble Ring Blade (`bubble-ring-blade`) has held `zOrder: 1`; the other seven
  weapons use `3`. Resolve approved source/catalogue attachment metadata and
  actual-facing compositions before changing it; avoid a per-name CSS patch.
- The two dark-line reports correspond to stable maze IDs `rainbow-power-parade`
  and `twilight-treasure-loop`. Their current themes include animated board
  decoration. That is one hypothesis to isolate alongside light masks, fog,
  culling and fractional transforms, **not an established cause**.
- The six art-review identities are `moon-bat`, `pebble-golem`, `hedgehog`,
  `alpaca`, `rainbow-horn-unicorn` and `penguin`. The new Human request permits
  this bounded review; it does not reopen Ame, Poggle, Sprig, Goblin, Violet Moon,
  branding, all other approved sprites or their provenance.
- The current Book candidate has a combined scrolling body and no persistent
  enemy-encounter ledger. Tabs and discovery therefore need a scoped future UI
  and save-contract slice, not an assertion that Agent 01 missed its frozen task.
  Today's visible ordinary Candy Mimic can be encountered normally; only future
  disguised chests require reveal. Preserve that discovery through a versioned
  ordinary-enemy/family alias when Plan 09 changes its role. Unsupported future
  profiles must not be overwritten by new Book writes. Surprise difficulty does
  not implicitly add generated active-run persistence.
