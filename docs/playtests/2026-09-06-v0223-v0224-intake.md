## Pickup glow/twinkle acceptance (V24-02)

- Use a low-opacity baseline and continuous eased modulation; no hard-off phase
  or bright pulse discontinuity. Preserve symbol, silhouette, Power text and
  material-local outlines. Full/Lite motion should not resemble a flickering sign.
- Choose colour from approved art/semantic metadata, not runtime pixel analysis.
  Gold/science/key/weapon identities remain distinct; colour is not the sole cue.
- Start with one key and one weapon in real gameplay. Prefer cached/shared
  low-cost halo art or a small fixed decoration layer whose opacity/transform
  animate, rather than animating a blur/filter on every source sprite. This is
  a candidate strategy, not a claim that compositing is free on WebKit.
- Bound on-screen twinkles and lifetime; cull/pause off-screen and hidden-page
  decoration; cap maximum simultaneous work across a crowded level. No one-rAF
  per item, per-frame React state, unbounded DOM or full-catalogue preload.
- Lite retains the readable base glow with fewer/no twinkles; Reduced avoids
  distracting repeated motion; Static is an attractive still frame. Follow
  existing preferences rather than inventing another quality system.
- Judge actual-size beauty and smoothness, then compare scenery-scroll cost,
  frame work, layer/decoded memory and worst-case pickups/followers against the
  same frozen baseline. A visually lovely small sample is not a performance pass.

## Authority changes and no-go boundaries

Wall-aware scatter replaces the prior no-wall cosmetic constraint for new work;
older approved results remain historical. Cosmetic loot cannot control engine RNG,
grant twice, disappear from durable totals, block input waiting on a stalled
animation, or alter required route Power without reviewed gameplay authority.
The Human's interest in permanent XP is captured for Plan14, not treated as an
approved economy in this implementation. Preserve normal campaign challenge and
single-player-first/co-op sequencing. Follow the joint state's single writer.
