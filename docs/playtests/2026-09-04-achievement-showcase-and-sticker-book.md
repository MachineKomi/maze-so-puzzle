# Playtest/product intake — achievement showcase and sticker book

- Date: 2026-09-04
- Source: direct Human feature request
- Status: captured for manager triage

## Earned-achievement showcase

Selecting an already-earned achievement should open a dedicated celebration
view with a large, high-resolution rendition of its approved achievement
sticker. The sticker should receive a beautiful rainbow holographic shimmer so
the player can pause and admire the reward. Locked achievements must not reveal
their full reward art through this interaction.

The effect should preserve the authored foil, enamel and holographic material
language rather than flattening it under a generic overlay. It must have a
reduced-motion/static presentation, work with touch, pointer, keyboard and
controller, and remain bounded enough for web, iPad and Tauri performance.

## Optional sticker book

As a lower-priority cosmetic feature, earned achievement stickers could be
placed freely into a two-page sticker book. After earning a sticker, the player
may choose to add it, select a position by touch/pointer or controller cursor,
and later move it. This is expressive play only: placement must not affect
progression, rewards, achievement truth or completion.

The feature requires an explicit later scope decision because free placement,
persistence, controller interaction, overlap/bounds behaviour and save migration
make it materially larger than the showcase viewer. The implementation should
not be slipped into an unrelated polish pass without an approved bounded design.
