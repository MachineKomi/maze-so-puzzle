## Room and maze variety

The four requested forms are distinct design promises, not four names for the
same generated layout. A monster room creates a compact encounter/planning
moment; a treasure room creates a rewarding discovery. A monster-led maze uses
guardian placement, attainable Power and route choices as its principal rhythm;
a treasure-led maze emphasises branching exploration, optional discoveries and
pleasant reward pacing. These can inhabit the existing 24-chapter programme and
Surprise Maze families without adding a new combat mode or another campaign.

Preserve ordinary completion, optional rescues and treasure, equal-Power wins,
harmless stronger guardians, safe returns and the revised Mimic safety policy.
Do not lock an arena until every enemy is defeated, require random loot, add
combat grind, or mistake a pile of identical enemies/chests for interesting
variety. Treasure supply and its visual/audio load remain bounded and feed the
Plan-10 Science/Egg audit. Room/maze profile, challenge level and topology are
separate authored/versioned decisions; a treasure maze can still contain a good
puzzle, and monster density alone does not make a maze Hard.

## Mechanics exploration, before campaign freeze

The request authorizes meaningful exploration, not automatic adoption of a new
rule. Compare stronger arrangements of existing rules with a small number of
new-rule hypotheses. A useful candidate has an immediately readable action and
consequence, a small teaching scene, a satisfying later combination, a safe
recovery, and a reason it improves the game more than its complexity costs.

Plan 09 records candidate benefits, failure cases and engine/solver/hint/save/
control/art/performance cost, then presents a concrete shortlist for the Human's
accept/defer/reject decision before dependent maps are frozen. Paper layouts or
static state diagrams can establish the question cheaply. New runtime mechanics
need a separately approved bounded specification, implementation owner,
migrations and requalification before campaign adoption. Spikes/ice from PT14
are still undecided; this general exploration request does not approve them.

Examples to examine, not chosen designs, are a clearly paired reversible switch
and gate, one pushable object with obvious safe undo, or the existing proposed
short ice-slide rule. Compare each with a clever key/portal/Power-order puzzle
that needs no extra rule. Reject mandatory precision timing, tedious resets,
hidden state or long explanations unless a separately demonstrated benefit
justifies an explicit Human decision. Preserve tutorial clarity and accessible
non-reading, non-colour and reduced/static communication.

Neither request is promised for FP-UI1. PT43 belongs to Plan 09 delivery; PT44's
exploration and explicit disposition belong to its preflight. A decision to
defer a particular new mechanic is recorded with a reason and possible Plan-14
return gate, rather than silently moving the whole exploration to after release.
