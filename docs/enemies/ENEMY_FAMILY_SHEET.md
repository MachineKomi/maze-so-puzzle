# Puzzlewild enemy family sheet

Status: Human-directed future art roster; source-only planning authority. No
entry in this document is a shipped runtime style, campaign placement,
animation state, combat rule, or approval of a generated candidate.

## Shared promise

Puzzlewild opponents are enthusiastic guardians, rivals and comic nuisances—not
evil targets. Every enemy obeys the Polite Sword Rule: a stronger guardian
blocks Ame kindly; an equal-or-lower-Power guardian makes a theatrical,
non-violent scoot aside. Weapons, fangs, horns, size and folklore identity are
visual challenge cues only. They never imply damage, chase, reach, charm,
constriction, drowning, poison, sacrifice or status effects.

All designs use a clear 40–84 px silhouette, two to four major colour masses,
three broad values, visible pupils, a readable friendly/competitive expression,
neutral front form modelling and visibly coloured material-local contours.
Field enemies never receive a cream sticker cutline or baked cast shadow.

The Human rejected all three original extension boards as a final family
rendering because they did not look like the selected core and current-family
art. The future-enemy default is therefore a new blank-canvas hybrid:

- Direction A current-family transfer controls rounded chunky construction,
  two-to-four masses, three connected values, detail ceiling, strong chroma and
  clean material-local contour weight.
- Direction B current-family transfer may contribute broad interior warmth,
  softly luminous colour relationships and one restrained gouache facet per
  material. It may not soften silhouettes or add microtexture.
- Core Direction C remains an explicit construction/optical exception for the
  Tea-Time Skeleton friend, Classic Slime and Lizard Sword Guard only. Lizard Spear
  later shares the approved lizard body sheet; Warrior Skeleton shares the
  approved toy-bone sheet.
- Contact-sheet cells never become identity masters, production sprites or
  future edit targets. Isolated approved sources must be authored independently
  after the rendering gate.

## Shared construction groups

- **Skeletons:** one approved toy-bone anatomy sheet; the Tea-Time Skeleton
  friend and Warrior Skeleton enemy share skull, rib, hand, foot, pivot and face
  grammar without sharing gameplay taxonomy.
- **Slimes:** one gel/material framework; classic uses the quietest mass, soda
  uses large bubbles and a straw, while existing berry/jelly identities remain
  separate.
- **Lizard guards:** one body/scale/tail sheet with weapon-specific silhouette
  and socket drawings. Sword and spear never become edit-of-edit descendants.
- **Mythic humanoids:** succubus, lamia, cyclops and minotaur require explicit
  child-safety and face/costume review before a source identity can be approved.
- **Large guardians:** T-Rex, Orc Chieftain, Cyclops and Minotaur still occupy
  the common one-tile registration envelope; apparent importance is not a new
  Power rule.
- **Folklore/object:** Kappa receives respectful folklore review. Treasure Mimic
  remains visibly alive because the game has no disguise/reveal mechanic.

## Production and provenance sequence

1. The historical rendering-direction gate compared Tea-Time Skeleton, Classic Slime,
   Lizard Sword Guard, Wholesome Succubus, Pocket T-Rex and Treasure Mimic.
2. After a rendering direction is approved, transfer-test Warrior Skeleton,
   Soda Slime, Lizard Spear Guard, Cultist and Kappa.
3. Produce Lamia, Orc Chieftain, Cyclops and Minotaur only after their relevant
   shared family construction is approved.
4. Each identity begins with exactly two independent runs from the same locked
   brief. Candidates never see one another. Contact-sheet crops are not masters.
5. Selected identities receive strict-v2 source records and transparent,
   registered 256/512 derivatives. The coloured contour is authored in the
   generator/source art; deterministic processing may cut out, register, resize
   and encode, but must not synthesize the art direction.

No IDs enter `ENEMY_STYLE_IDS`, `ENEMY_ART`, `ENEMY_PERSONALITIES`, Surprise
generation, or campaign levels at this gate. Runtime inclusion requires an
explicit visual-roster migration so old Surprise seeds do not silently change,
plus Plan 09 placement and the existing gameplay/contracts test suite.

## Review checklist

- Recognisable and emotionally safe within five seconds at 40 px.
- Challenge reads through silhouette/posture rather than menace.
- Face and pupils survive grayscale and colour-vision simulation.
- No more than four major colour masses or three broad values.
- Long contours remain visibly material-coloured at 64 px; pure black is zero.
- `ink-900` is confined to face/deep occlusion, never a default perimeter.
- No cream sticker halo, floor shadow, directional cast shadow or VFX baked in.
- Transparent edge, registration, decoded-memory and encoded-byte checks pass.
- Public labels for Succubus and Cultist receive explicit Human/rating review.
